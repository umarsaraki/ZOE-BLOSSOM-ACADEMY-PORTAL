Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Authorized Access Only

This portal is strictly for registered Students, Teachers, and Administrators of the school. Access is granted only through a valid Registration Number and Password issued by the school.

Prohibited Access

Attempting to log in, guess credentials, or otherwise gain access to this system without authorization is strictly prohibited. Such conduct may constitute an offence under the Nigerian Cybercrimes (Prohibition, Prevention, etc.) Act, and may be reported to law enforcement for investigation and possible prosecution.

Data Use

Information entered into this portal is used solely for school administration purposes and is not shared with third parties except as required by law.

Account Responsibility

Users are responsible for keeping their password confidential and must not share their login credentials with any other person.
